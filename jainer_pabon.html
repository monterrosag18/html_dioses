INTRODUCTION TO THE WORLD

The World of Serankua: Immerse Yourself in the Law of the Great Creator

Serankua (also known as Seiyankua or Seizhankua) is the revered Creator Father and original Mamo in the Kogui worldview of Colombia, a being of ancestral wisdom who resides in the spiritual heart of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta. His story is the narrative of how Thought, not force, shaped the universe. Before material existence, Serankua emerged from the vast darkness of Alúna, the Primordial Spirit or Mind. Through deep meditation, Serankua began to “weave” the cosmos, conceiving the nine worlds and giving structure to our Earth, Sé Nenulang, imbuing it with life. He is the divine architect who shaped the snowy peaks, sacred lagoons, and valleys of the Sierra, making it the center of the world and a living, breathing being.
The most crucial act of Serankua was the delivery of the Law of Origin, a sacred mandate that is not a simple moral code, but the very design of cosmic harmony. This Law teaches the Elder Brothers (the indigenous peoples of the Sierra) to honor Mother Earth, maintain vital balance, and “repay” (through offerings and thought) for the sustenance nature provides. Serankua left his legacy in the Mamos, his descendants, who continue interpreting the Law to ensure the divine order remains intact, protecting the world from chaos and the exploitation of those who have forgotten the Creator's original thought. His story is an eternal lesson in reciprocity and the defense of life's balance.

What makes it special?

The world of Serankua is a role-playing game where the planet itself is the main character! What makes it unique is that balance is the most important game mechanic. Our Sierra Nevada is not just a map; it is the "Heart of the World," a living being that feels and reacts to every action. Here, knowledge and meditation (the powers of the Mamos) are worth more than gold or strength. Players must navigate the world understanding that every resource taken or every rule broken reduces the "Health of Alúna," which can trigger the wrath of the Zaiku spirits. It's an adventure where your greatest enemy is not a monster, but the imbalance created by greed and the neglect of the Law of Origin.

Catalog

Places

The Cosmic Matrix: The Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta is the matrix, the point of origin. Key locations include the four snow-capped peaks (the pillars of the sky), the Sacred Lagoons (centers of meditation and healing), and Teyuna (Lost City), an ancient ruin and a place of great spiritual power.

Characters

Mamo Class (Main NPC): Heirs of Serankua, they are the Spiritual Guides (High Priests). They possess the highest Level of Wisdom. Other important NPCs include the Ancestral Mothers (keepers of species and elements) and the Zaiku (Avenging Spirits of imbalance).

Artifacts

The Poporo (Wisdom Container): A spiritual toolkit that every character must have. It is a gourd with lime (obtained from seashells) used with a stick to chew coca and meditate. It symbolizes the maturity of thought. The Staff of Command grants ceremonial authority.

The Creation in Nine Levels

Maps of Cosmic Exploration: The universe is a Cosmic Egg with nine layers. The Earth is on the Fifth Level. The lower levels belong to Darkness, and the upper levels to Light. The Mamos can travel through these levels in their meditations.

The Golden Thread of Balance

World Health Meter: It represents the invisible connection that Serankua established. When the "Younger Brothers" exploit the Earth, the Thread tightens and can break. If it breaks completely, Chaos ensues and the world disintegrates.

The Payment Sanctuaries

Mandatory Mission Zones: Specific locations (mountains, lagoons, stones) designated by Serankua to perform the Payments and restore balance. Ignoring a Sanctuary increases the player's overall imbalance level.

The Word (El Sai)

Ability Power: Serankua used the Sai (The Word/The Thought) to shape the world. The Mamos use the Sai to heal, meditate, and "weave" reality anew. It is the most powerful skill in the game; it does not require strength, only concentration.

Rules

The Law of Origin (Origin Code): The only set of rules. The core mechanic is the "Payment" or "Yuluka." Each time you take a resource or cause harm, you must perform a ceremonial Payment to restore spiritual balance. Failing to pay causes Chaos (penalties).

SYSTEM

System Rules

  1. Mandatory Reciprocity: You cannot take without giving. Every resource taken (water, wood, food) must be compensated with a Payment (Yuluka).
  2. Chaos Level: This metric rises with exploitation and decreases with Payments and meditation. If it reaches 100%, you lose the game.
  3. Zaiku Punishment: A high Chaos Level attracts the Avenging Spirits (Zaiku), who impose severe penalties.
  4. Meditation (El Sai): The primary tool to gain Wisdom Points (XP) and reduce imbalance.
  5. Time of Thought: Important actions can only be performed after a period of deep reflection (similar to the Mamo ceremonies).

Internal Rules

  1. Respect for the Sanctuaries: Never desecrate a Sacred Lagoon or a snow-capped peak.
  2. Consult the Mamo: Every decision affecting the Sierra must be consulted with the Mamo before acting.
  3. Prohibition of Greed: Unnecessary accumulation of resources is the greatest violation of the Law of Origin.
  4. The Secret of the Offerings: Payments are private. They are not made for display, but for the spiritual healing of the world.
  5. The Secret of the Offerings: Payments are private. They are not made for display, but for the spiritual healing of the world.

Hierarchies

  1. Serankua: The Creator (Level 0: Absolute Origin).
  2. Alúna: The Spirit/Mind from which all emanates (Divine Substance).
  3. Mamos: Priests/Spiritual Leaders (Highest class on Earth).
  4. Elder Brothers (Kogui, Arhuaco, etc.): Guardians of the Law.
  5. Younger Brothers (External Civilization): Players with Chronic Chaos Level.

Phases

  1. Phase 1: The Original Thought (Serankua): The mythical era of perfect creation.
  2. Phase 2: The Imbalance (The Arrival of the Younger Brother): The beginning of exploitation and the breaking of the Golden Thread.
  3. Phase 3: Meditation (The Role of the Mamo): The current phase, where the Mamos strive to restore order.
  4. Phase 4: The Great Payment: The final phase, where humanity must make a massive Payment to prevent total Chaos.
  5. Phase 5: The New Cycle: If balance is achieved, the world is reborn. If it fails, the world disintegrates.

The Law of Origin and the Veins of the Earth

  1. The Cycles of Purification: The world self-cleanses through floods and droughts if the Imbalance is too high.
  2. The Power of Stories: Telling the myths of Serankua is an act of magic that strengthens the Law of Origin.
  3. The Burden of Consciousness: The Mamos carry the world's guilt, performing the "Payment" for the sins of others.
  4. The Nourishment of the Spirit: The poporo with lime and coca is not for recreation; it is the necessary "food" for mental work.
  5. The Seven Lines of Life: The veins of the Earth traced by Serankua; if they are cut (dams, mining), Mother Earth weakens.

SUPPLEMENTARY CONTENT

Curiosities

  • The Sierra Nevada is the highest coastal mountain in the world, a geographic marvel that corresponds to its mythical status.
  • Knowledge is not written; it is memorized and transmitted orally over years of isolation and meditation by the future Mamo.
  • Serankua is sometimes seen as a sun and other times as a primordial shamanic ancestor.
  • The white clothing of the Kogui is not just tradition; it symbolizes purity, the cold of the snow, and clear thought.
  • The concept of "progress" of the Younger Brothers is seen as a disease by the Mamos.

Glossary

  • Alúna: The Spirit, the Mind, the metaphysical Substance of the universe.
  • Sé Nenulang: The Earth, the inhabited world.
  • Mamo: Priest/Spiritual Leader. The living interpreter of Serankua.
  • Zaiku: Powerful nature spirits that punish violations of the Law.
  • Yuluka: The "Payment" or obligatory ceremonial offering to maintain balance.
