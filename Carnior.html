Introduction to the World of Solmarion

Solmarion is a realm of light, rhythmic tides, and cosmic energy. Its god, Carnior, dances between constellations and oceans, guiding the vibration of the universe.

What makes this world special is its connection between rhythm and destiny: every tide, star, and creature responds to the divine pulse of Carnior.

System and Rules of Solmarion

  • The light-tide flows between oceans and constellations.
  • The tides respond to Carnior’s divine rhythm.
  • The Tide Guardians protect the ancient temples.
  • Light portals connect distant regions.
  • The constellations change according to the world’s pulse.

Glossary

  • Light-tide: Vital energy of the world.
  • Rhythmic Nexuses: Rhythms that connect regions.
  • Tide Guardians: Protective order.
  • Arcs of Solmare: Light portals.

Curiosities

  • The sea glows turquoise every night.
  • Solmarion never has complete darkness.
  • The constellations change with the tides.
